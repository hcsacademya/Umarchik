# Rasm promptlari (2-bosqich)

**Qoidalar**
- Avvalgi chatda davom eting (o'sha yerda asosiy g'oya bor). Yangi chat ochsangiz, avval 1-bosqichdagi "ASOSIY G'OYA" matnini yuboring.
- Har bir promptdan oldin shu eslatmani yuboring:

```
Follow the fixed concept and sprite sheet rules from my first message exactly.
```

- **Burchak haqida:** hamma narsa **yon tomondan** chiziladi (Grow Empire: Rome o'yinidagidek). Qasr darvozasi **o'ngga**, jang maydoniga qaraydi. Dushman qasrini kod o'zi ko'zgudek aylantiradi.
- **Tartib:** 1 → 2 → 3 → 4 → 5–8 → 9–16. 1-rasm natijasini saqlab qo'ying, keyingi qasr, kon va fon promptlarida uni uslub uchun biriktirasiz.
- **Ko'k rang qoidasi:** dushman qasrlari va dushman jangchilarida mato qismlari **toza ko'k** bo'ladi. Kod har jangda shu ko'kni boshqa rangga (qizil, sariq, yashil va hokazo) almashtiradi. Shuning uchun boshqa joyda ko'k bo'lmasligi kerak.

## 1. Bizning qasr: yon tomondan, 4 holat
Rasm biriktirish: **HA**, avvalgi qasr rasmini (faqat uslub va material uchun).
```
Using the same art style and materials as the attached castle, draw OUR castle for a side-scrolling battle game (like the fortress in "Grow Empire: Rome"). Camera: three-quarter SIDE view. The castle's front wall with the main gate faces RIGHT toward the battlefield and is turned about 40 degrees toward the viewer, so the gate opening is still visible; the rest of the castle (towers, walls, keep) extends back to the LEFT in perspective. Sandstone walls, round towers, crenellations, a wall-walk where archers can stand, blue-and-gold banners. The castle is wider than tall (about 5:3).
2x2 grid of equal cells, the SAME castle in four states:
1) intact, gate OPEN (dark passage visible inside)
2) intact, gate CLOSED (heavy wooden gate shut)
3) damaged: cracked walls, broken battlements, one tower top collapsed, scorch marks, arrows stuck in the walls
4) ruined: collapsed into rubble and charred beams, only stumps of the towers remain
Same size and position in every cell, bottom edges on the same line, nothing cropped.
```

## Tekshirish (har rasmdan keyin)
- Qasr va kon **yon tomondan** chizilganmi? Qasr darvozasi o'ngga, kon kirish joyi chapga qaraganmi?
- Jangchilar **o'ngga** qaraganmi, oyoqlari bir chiziqda turibdimi?
- Dushmanda ko'k rang **faqat** mato va qalqonda ishlatilganmi?
- Fon kulrang va bir xil, soya va yer yo'qmi? Bu jang foni rasmlariga tegishli emas.

Tayyor rasmlarni chatga tashlang, men tekshirib, o'yinga qo'yaman.
